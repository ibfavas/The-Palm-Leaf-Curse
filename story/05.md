### Chapter 5: The Confession

The message arrives from Meera’s number three hours after the police find her.

“Stop reading.”

The words do not sound like her. You read them again, then look at the room as if the person who sent them might be standing just outside the door. Meera is gone. Someone has her phone.

The power fails before dawn. Rain taps the glass while your phone throws a weak circle of light across a confession written more than two centuries ago. Its pages have waited longer than any of the people named in it.
