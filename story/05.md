### Chapter 5: The Confession

Her name stays at the top of your screen. The words do not sound like her. You read them again, searching for the warmth that used to reach you through her messages. Meera is gone. Someone has her phone.

The power fails before dawn. Your phone throws a weak circle of light over the old confession before you. Its pages have waited longer than any of the people named in them. You are alone now. The rain keeps tapping at the glass. Who is holding Meera’s phone?
