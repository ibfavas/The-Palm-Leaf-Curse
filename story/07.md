### Chapter 7: The Last Page

Professor Raghavan’s desk is gone from the archive photograph, but you can still picture the lamp burning over the open manuscript. His last night left behind a roomful of ordinary things: a ledger, a phone, a machine that no one thought to ask about until now.

You keep returning to the professor’s final note to Meera. The ink was steady. He believed she would have time to read it.

The rain has thinned to a mist. Somewhere in the archive, a bundle of palm leaves shifts against its cord.
