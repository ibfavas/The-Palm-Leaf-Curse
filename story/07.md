### Chapter 7: The Last Page

The police hand you Professor Raghavan’s laptop. It rests on your desk, ordinary and closed. His last night left behind a ledger, a phone, and a machine no one thought to ask about until now.

You can still picture the lamp burning over the open manuscript in the archive. The rain has thinned to mist over Thrissur. Somewhere inside the locked building, a bundle of palm leaves shifts against its cord.

The doors are shut. There is no wind in the room. What made the leaves move?
