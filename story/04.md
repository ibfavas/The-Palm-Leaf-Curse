### Chapter 4: The Silence

Meera's last message arrives at 1:14 a.m. “There’s a car outside my house. It’s been there for an hour. The lights are off.” A moment later she sends a second text: “I’m probably being dramatic. I had tea and I’m fine. Stay with me a little longer?”

You answer. The message is delivered. Meera does not read it.

Morning comes without sleep. You call once; the phone rings into silence. You call again and hang up before voicemail. The rain keeps the street outside the window empty and silver.

By noon, you have worn a path between the window and the desk. The professor’s papers sit where you left them. Meera’s last message remains bright on the phone, the small typing bubble never returning.
