### Chapter 6: The Watcher

Kunnathu Properties presents its township in bright photographs: new roads, clean glass, green fields without a single person in them. The project is planned for land that once belonged to the temple.

Devan Kunnathu’s portrait sits above the company name. He looks composed, almost kind. The image has the stillness of a photograph taken after everyone else has left the room.

On your desk, the old letters and the new plans lie side by side. The same family name joins them. Outside, a car waits with its lights off.
