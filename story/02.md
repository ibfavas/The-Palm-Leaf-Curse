### Chapter 2: The First Page

The scan fills your screen: a long, darkened leaf, its edges worn smooth by generations of hands. Fine lines of Kolezhuthu run from one end to the other. The stylus marks catch the light and disappear again when you move the page.

There is no colour in the old writing, only the brown of the leaf and the black of its grooves. You imagine the professor bent over the original beneath his brass lamp, listening to the rain on the archive roof.

The room around you is quiet. Rain fills the space between each line.
