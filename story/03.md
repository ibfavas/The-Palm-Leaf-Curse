### Chapter 3: The Archive

The archive is quiet enough to hear monsoon rain ticking against the window. In the staff photographs, the oldest rooms are all damp stone, locked cabinets, and glass that gives back more darkness than it lets through. The pale rectangle of your screen floats over each pane.

You picture Raghavan beneath the brass lamp, alone among those shelves while Thrissur sleeps. He turns one leaf at a time. The rest of the building holds its breath around him.

Meera texts: “You’re still awake, aren’t you?” You answer yes. “Okay,” she writes. “I’m not ready to sleep yet either.” For a few minutes, neither of you has to explain why.

Your phone gives a soft, brief vibration. The screen wakes, then goes dark. No new message. You wait for it to happen again.
