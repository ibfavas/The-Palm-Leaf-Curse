### Chapter 3: The Archive

The archive is quiet enough to hear the rain tick against the window. Its oldest rooms appear in the staff photographs: damp stone, locked cabinets, glass that reflects more darkness than it lets through.

Professor Raghavan spent years among those shelves. You picture him alone beneath the brass lamp, turning one page at a time while the rest of the building slept.

Your phone gives a soft, brief vibration. There is no new message on the screen.
