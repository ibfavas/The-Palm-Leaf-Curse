### Chapter 1: The Professor's Desk

The State Archives look ordinary in the hard blue light of your monitor. Their pages are orderly, the photographs carefully framed, every sentence composed to outlast the person who wrote it.

Professor Raghavan Menon appears in an old staff portrait. He sits at a desk crowded with ledgers and tied palm leaves, one hand resting beside an open book. His expression is not quite a smile. Rain has silvered the window behind him.

The manuscript record is marked in red: **DO NOT DIGITISE.** The warning is so cleanly printed that it seems less like an instruction than a fear someone tried to make official.
