### Prologue: The Call

11 pm. Rain on the roof. Your phone buzzes. A voice message from a number you do not know. A girl, whispering, like she is afraid the walls can hear her.

"My name is Meera Menon. My grandfather is dead. The police say heart attack, but he was fine, he was working, and the manuscript was open on his desk. He was the only person in fifty years who could read it. Everyone who read it died. Don't call me back. Text only. Someone is watching my grandfather's house. Will you help me?"

You say yes. She sends you the address of the archives' digitisation portal and the professor's login.

The lights flicker. Somewhere in the building, something rustles, like dry leaves in a room with no wind.

Everyone who read it died. You are reading it now.
