### Prologue: The Call

At eleven, rain drums on the roof. In the hard blue light of your monitor, your phone jumps against the desk. The number is unknown. A voice message waits.

A young woman whispers, as if the walls might carry her words. “My name is Meera Menon. My grandfather is dead. The police say heart attack, but he was fine. He was working, and the manuscript was open on his desk. He was the only person in fifty years who could read it. Everyone who read it died.”

Her breath catches. “Please don’t call me back. Text only. Someone is watching my grandfather’s house. Will you help me?”

You answer yes. A second message brings the archive’s digitisation portal address and the professor’s login.

The lights flicker. Somewhere in the building, dry leaves rustle in a room without wind.

Everyone who read it died. The rain keeps tapping at the glass. You are reading it now.
