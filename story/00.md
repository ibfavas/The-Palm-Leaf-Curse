### Prologue: The Call

11:07 p.m. Rain threads the dark window. Your phone lights up with a voice message from an unknown number. The woman on it sounds young, tired, and careful with every word.

“I’m Meera Menon. I’m studying journalism. My grandfather, Professor Raghavan, died at his desk last night. The police say his heart stopped. He was working until the end, and his manuscript was open in front of him. He was the last person in our family who could read it.”

There is a small pause. When Meera speaks again, her voice has softened. “I know how this sounds. I don’t believe in curses. I just want to know what happened to him. Could you help me understand what he left behind?”

She asks you to text, never call. A second message arrives with the archive account details. Behind you, the room settles with a dry, papery rustle. The windows are shut.
