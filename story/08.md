### Epilogue: The Truth

You send everything to the police. The confession, the priest's letter, the emails, the photograph of yourself, the professor's last note.

Devan Kunnathu is arrested. The fixer is named. The deed is challenged in court, the township project stalls, and the manuscript is authenticated by the university and put on public display, the first time anyone has been allowed to read it in fifty years.

The last line of the manuscript, in Kunjiraman's own hand, reads:

> "I write this so that the truth outlives the men who buried it."

A week later, your phone lights up. Meera's number.

"thank you."

Meera is dead. Nobody can explain the message. The police say her phone was in an evidence locker when it was sent.

In the archives, the manuscript sits in a glass case. The archivist swears the leaves rustle at night, when the building is empty and the doors are locked.

You never open the manuscript again.
